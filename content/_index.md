Imagine this: your build finishes, and the only difference between "passed" and "failed" is red versus green.

For a lot of people, that difference just... isn't there. 🤷

This site is a catalog of color themes and palettes built for people with color vision deficiency (CVD), aka color blindness. Every theme links to its source, shows its real hex values, and comes with a simulation plate showing how it looks under each type of CVD.

I also ran every one of them through a simulator and scored it. Some did great. Some did... less great. The scores are in every table, and [here's how I get them](verification/).

If you're impatient, [jump straight to all the themes](all-themes/). If you want to know what CVD actually is first, [start here](what-is-cvd/).
